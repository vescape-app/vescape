# Alert repetition is explicit per rule, not a global debounce

The alert engine debounced every non-battery rule with a blanket 10s `lastFiredAt` cooldown, so a metric parked past a threshold re-announced forever at 10s intervals — a motor holding 71°C on a climb spoke every 10 seconds, three preset points at once. Battery alone had the correct model: an arm/re-arm latch plus a 10 percentage-point margin.

We generalized the latch to every single-threshold **Alert Rule** and deleted the cooldown. A rule announces on crossing, then stays silent until an **Alert Re-Arm** — the metric falling back past its threshold by a per-metric margin (temperature 3°C, duty 5pp, speed 3km/h, battery unchanged at 10pp). Repetition, where it is wanted, is a rider-chosen `repeatEverySeconds` on the rule (floor 3s) rather than an engine-wide default. Rules that fire together on one metric in the same evaluation coalesce to the most severe; the rest latch silently, so a fast climb produces one announcement instead of a stutter of text-to-speech cut off mid-word. The latch clears only on a new **Board Session**, not on rule edits, preset regeneration, or idle pause.

Rejected: a relative hysteresis (one percentage of threshold for all metrics) — it degenerates for battery, where 5% of a 30% threshold is a 1.5pp margin that a resting-voltage wobble crosses. Also rejected: per-rule cooldowns, which keep the noisy default and hide it behind a knob nobody sets. Consequence worth knowing: the engine has no time-based safety net left, so any future rule flavor must arrive with its own re-arm condition or it will announce on every telemetry tick.
