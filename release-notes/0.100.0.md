## New

- Spoken weather alerts warn of approaching storms, rain and strong gusts while your board is connected. Choose rain and wind sensitivity in Weather settings.
- Choose alert effects and voices, adjust how much they say, or import your own sounds. Spoken preset alerts report current readings, and custom alerts can run alongside presets.
- Personalize your board's rail model and color. Board presets also fill in battery settings during setup.
- History now shows ride weather and filters by date, board and conditions. Select multiple rides to export together as a ZIP or delete, with Floaty export joining GPX and CSV.
- See today's distance and ride time beside the History button, open daily ride summaries, and tap profile records to revisit the rides that set them.
- Choose distance and temperature units independently, including miles with Celsius.

## Improved

- Critical alert audio takes priority over weather and social announcements, which play one at a time.
- Navigation recalculates when you stray beyond your chosen reroute distance, fades the path already ridden, and shows estimated time remaining. Automatic recalculation preserves your map view.
- Tune selection now summarizes aggression, turning and ATR, with your personalized board shown in Tune Preview.

## Fixed

- Corrected battery percentage compensation under load for several cell presets.
- Short connection interruptions no longer split rides in History, while long gaps in recorded board data separate them.
- Improved light-theme contrast across controls, tabs and map overlays.

## Watch

- Add a board battery complication to your Apple Watch or Wear OS watch face. It keeps the selected board's last known charge available after disconnection, with reading age shown in supported layouts.
