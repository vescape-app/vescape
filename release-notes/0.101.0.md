## New

- Send group ride signals for injuries, board trouble, a wait or a break, and mark hazards for your crew. Signals appear on the map with spoken announcements and Ride Notices; hazards warn riders as they approach. Sending inside a Privacy Zone warns you that the pin shares your location.
- Browse your board's Refloat settings and motor limits by topic, search for options, and view the last saved configuration while disconnected.
- Choose 12-hour or 24-hour time in Units settings.

## Improved

- Group ride members stay visible as Away when their connection drops. The roster keeps a stable order, and brief disconnects no longer trigger repeated join and leave sounds.
- Recorded ride data uses less storage, with existing recordings converted automatically in the background.
- The map glides back to your location when leaving History, with smoother transitions over longer distances.

## Fixed

- Corrected the swapped default speed and duty alert sounds.
- Free-spinning wheels are now detected from board readings without relying on GPS, keeping them out of recorded average speed, maximum speed and maximum duty.
- Fixed tune writes hanging on boards whose Bluetooth modules cannot handle large messages.
- Fixed map searches failing and offscreen indicators remaining visible after their target returned onscreen.

## Watch

- Rider signals now appear as full-screen Ride Notices with a vibration on Apple Watch and Wear OS. Tap to dismiss the notice on both watch and phone and return to the gauges.
