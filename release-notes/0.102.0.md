## New

- Public Rides replace group ride rooms. Share your location for a chosen duration or whenever your board connects, with manual sharing available without a board. Find nearby riders on the map without joining a room.
- Set your rider nick and country flag, search for riders, add friends, and see when they’re riding. Rider profiles show stats, live ride details, and friends’ recent public rides.
- Ride History now shows who you rode with and lets you display their shared tracks alongside yours.

## Improved

- Board configuration notices skip routine calibration changes. You can also ignore individual fields per board and restore their notices later from Board Config options.

## Fixed

- Privacy Zone sizes now match the area shown on the editor map.
- The board visualization and pitch markers now tilt in the correct direction when the nose rises or falls.

## Watch

- During a Public Ride, see up to ten nearby riders on your wrist, with direction, distance, and board status, without joining a group ride room.
