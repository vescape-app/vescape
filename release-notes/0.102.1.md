## New

- Delete your Vescape account from the app, with a clear explanation of what is removed. Your boards, ride history, favorites, tunes, and settings stay on your phone.
- Choose your preferred date format in Settings → Units, including day-first, month-first, and ISO formats.

## Improved

- Refloat tune writes now require recent live data showing the board idle and out of handtest. Values the board cannot store are rejected before writing.
- Save & sync sends only the tune fields you just edited, leaving other board settings alone and showing any remaining differences.
- Your Celsius or Fahrenheit preference now applies consistently to board temperatures, live and ride-history charts, weather, and temperature alert thresholds and spoken messages.

## Fixed

- Fixed Refloat tune values being written incorrectly, false verification failures, and retries using outdated board settings after an interrupted write. Tune syncing also preserves recent light changes on Refloat versions through 1.2.1.
- Account controls remain accessible if the account connection fails, so you can retry, sign out, or delete your account.

## Watch

- Apple Watch and Wear OS now follow your phone’s Celsius or Fahrenheit preference for motor, controller, and weather temperatures, including hourly forecasts.
