## New

- Android riders can create or join nearby Group Rides, see live rider locations and board status on the map, and keep their location private inside enabled Privacy Zones.
- Profile stats now show all-time and monthly distance, ride count, ride time, speeds, longest ride, and battery energy totals.

## Improved

- Ride Recording automatically pauses while idle and resumes as soon as you start moving again, with a clear paused state on the recording control.

## Fixed

- Android auto-start rides can now retain their GPS track in the background, with guidance for granting the required location permission.
