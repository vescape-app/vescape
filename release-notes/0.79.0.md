## New

- Vescape now verifies your board's firmware, Refloat package, and BMS after connecting. Firmware commands remain blocked until the saved Board Link is trusted, and hardware or firmware changes prompt you to re-link.
- Tune Preview lets you compare how Tune Profiles respond to speed, pitch, hills, and ATR before syncing them to your board.

## Improved

- Smart-BMS details now follow the ride scrubber and show peak cell spread, the worst cell group, balancing, and charging state.
- Tune Profiles can now have their own icon and color, and are easier to select from the main screen.
- Photos and videos added to a ride are now kept with that ride and remain available in its gallery and map without ongoing photo-library access.
- Manually closing Vescape can now pause Auto Start, preventing the app from immediately reopening.

## Fixed

- Compass-follow navigation is available again with a smoother, more efficient map-rendering path.
