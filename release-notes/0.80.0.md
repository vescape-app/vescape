## New

- Board Warnings now monitor for unsafe configuration and battery conditions, including disabled footpad sensing, late pushback thresholds, disabled moving-fault protection, excessive cell spread, and battery configuration mismatches. Warnings explain the risk and can be dismissed or restored.
- A new Auto Close option can close Vescape after your board remains disconnected for a configurable time.

## Improved

- The Wear OS companion can now open automatically when your board connects, shows clearer live gauges and connection state, and includes on-watch diagnostics.
- Ride History now provides a clearer empty state before your first recorded ride.
