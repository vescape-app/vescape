## Improved

- Automatic ride recording is now enabled by default for new setups.
- Map movement is smoother when following your ride, dragging to reveal the map, recentering, focusing on riders or points, and viewing ride history.

## Fixed

- Live telemetry readouts no longer disappear on iOS or cause rapid-update failures on Android.
- The Android board notification now stays in sync through stale telemetry, reconnection, errors, and disconnects. It no longer shows old values and keeps Disconnect available during recovery.
- Map movement no longer overshoots or vibrates after dragging to reveal the map.
