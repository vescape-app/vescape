## New

- Move your board forward or backward from Board Settings with hold-to-move controls, adjustable strength, trusted-link checks, and an immediate stop on release.
- Open a new Settings drawer from the ride screen for quick access to account status, app updates, storage, and frequently used settings.
- Choose how long a stop must be before Vescape splits a ride; changes also regroup past rides.

## Improved

- Alerts now re-arm only after telemetry returns to a safe margin, preventing repeated warnings near a threshold. Alerts can also repeat at a chosen interval, use one to five beeps, and clearly show sustained warning ranges on gauges.
- Temperature presets now better reflect motor and controller limits, with repeating warnings near critical temperatures and gauges extending to 100°C.

## Fixed

- Legal Mode errors remain visible while the modal closes, giving you enough time to read what went wrong.
- Drawers now dismiss with a smoother, more consistent fade.
