## New

- Move a disengaged board forward or backward from your phone or Wear OS watch with hold-to-move controls. Choose the strength; releasing stops immediately, and wrist movement stops automatically if commands are interrupted.
- Plan a route from your live position to a map destination, choose paths, cycleways, or roads, and review its distance and duration before riding. Follow remaining distance on your phone, with the route and optional direction arrow mirrored to Wear OS.
- View current conditions and an hourly forecast on Wear OS, including rain chances and sunrise and sunset times.
- Stop an active ride directly from the iOS Lock Screen or expanded Dynamic Island after authentication.
- Open account status, updates, storage, and frequently used settings from the new ride-screen Settings drawer.
- Choose how long a stop must last before Vescape splits a ride; changing it also regroups past rides.

## Improved

- Alerts now re-arm only after telemetry returns to a safe margin, preventing repeated warnings near a threshold. Each rule can repeat at a chosen interval or use one to five beeps, while sustained warning ranges are clearer on gauges.
- Live gauges now scale speed to the active board’s top speed and extend temperature scales to 100°C.
- The Wear OS mirror uses less battery during long rides by entering ambient mode and reducing unnecessary phone-link work.
- Map search now favors nearby results and uses icons that better match each place, while enabled privacy zones appear directly on the map.
- Tune now presents Basic settings first and keeps Tune Preview and advanced values hidden until requested.

## Fixed

- Board scans on iOS now continue when system UI briefly makes the app inactive instead of stopping before the board appears.
