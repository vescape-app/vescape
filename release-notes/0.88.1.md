## Improved

- Live telemetry charts now keep related readings synchronized while zooming and scrubbing, making battery, footpad, and IMU data easier to compare at the same moment.
- Empty telemetry readouts now show their units and hide unavailable maximums, making disconnected screens easier to understand.

## Fixed

- Bottom drawers now stay fully visible when their content changes and remain stable during drag gestures.
- Chart scrubbing now reports the closest recorded value when telemetry samples are sparse.
