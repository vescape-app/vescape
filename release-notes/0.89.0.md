## New

- History now opens with an overview of your riding stats, recent rides, favorites, and route previews, with quick access to complete ride lists.
- Group Ride now works on iPhone with live rider positions, roster updates, reconnection, and background continuity during active rides.

## Improved

- Alert presets now explain what you’ll hear at each level and make it easier to preview alert sounds before riding.
- Ride History now loads complete rides in reliable pages, keeping long histories and profile stats consistent.
- Auto-connect on iPhone now starts when Vescape launches and avoids duplicate connections when restoring an active session.

## Fixed

- Board Move now runs steadily instead of pulsing on boards using Refloat 1.0–1.2.
- Opening a ride or favorite from History now selects the correct item, and favorite route thumbnails render reliably.
- Compass heading on iPhone now points in the correct direction and stays stable during rides; off-screen map indicators no longer twitch in compass mode.
- Weather radar now opens near current conditions and displays consistent 24-hour times.
