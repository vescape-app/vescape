## Fixed

- Fixed an app error when saved sign-in credentials temporarily cannot be read.
- Fixed hard-to-read disabled buttons in light mode and GPS status colors that did not update when switching themes.

## Watch

- Added Apple Watch support with live speed, duty cycle, battery and temperature gauges, plus your route, distance to destination, weather forecast and rain radar. You can also control board lights and hold to move the board from your wrist. Move stops when you release or the connection is lost. Open Vescape on the watch manually to get started.
- Adjusted the Wear OS weather layout to keep the forecast and sunrise or sunset time visible on smaller screens.
