## Watch

- Added Remote Tilt control on Apple Watch and Wear OS. Drag up or down to adjust tilt, release to hold it, and double-tap to ease back to neutral. Tilt stays set when the watch sleeps or disconnects. The gauges show active tilt, and Board Move warns when moving will clear it. Adjust the stick's speed in Watch settings on your phone.
